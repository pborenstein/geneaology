## Conventions

- **IDs** (stable, first-seen order): `A##` Borenstein line · `B##` Mostkoff line · `C##` Polak/Borukovich line · `X##` unplaced/other-affiliation. People are indexed in their **birth line** where known (Shifra Boruchovich → C); spouses without a line marry into their spouse's line.
- **Confidence** (DEC-003): `[C]` confirmed by record · `[L]` likely (source or strong inference) · `[?]` open question.
- **Citations** (short keys; page = PDF page of the file in `base-documents/`):
  - `Narrative, p. N` = *Mostkoff Family Narrative.pdf* (master). **NB: pp. 38–71 duplicate pp. 1–37** (two embedded copies of the same text); first-copy pages cited.
  - `Notes, p. N` = *Borenstein Mostkoff Notes.pdf*
  - `Tanya-summary, p. N` = *Summarized Belarus memories of Tanya Mostkoff.pdf*
  - `Genealogy draft, p. N` = *Borenstein-Mostkoff Family Genealogy.pdf*
  - `chronicle, p. N` · `Slutsk Links, p. N` · `MOSTKOFF, p. N` · `SUPONITZKY, p. N` · `timeline, p. N` = same-named PDFs
  - `TM (…)` = Tania's memoir as quoted/summarized inside another source; `tania-project (§…)` = full English translation at tania-project.com (docs/RESOURCES.md)
  - `EV-##` = record in `data/evidence-log.md`; anything else cited by URL verbatim.
- **Aliases**: every person keeps all known variants. Never merge same-named people without corroboration (CLAUDE.md rule).
- Where sources disagree, entries cite both sides and reference `data/conflicts.md` (CF-##).

## Line B — Mostkoff (Ostrov/Slutsk, Belarus → Mexico City)

### B01 · Aryeh Leib "Leibe" Mostkoff (Lev; Leiba; Leon?)
- b. ~1855–60, "about the same time as Chaya" (Wendy's proposal from children's dates) `[L]` Narrative, p. 5. Poor man; "what her husband Leiba was doing, I don't know… He was a poor man" `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`.
- Married **Chaya Sapotnitsky** ~1875–76 `[L]` Narrative, p. 5.
- Israel's headstone reads "Israel son of Aryeh Leib" `[C]` Narrative, p. 6 → Leibe = Aryeh Leib; TM: grandfather "Lev, died early, leaving mother and children alone" `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`.
- d. before 1914 (birth of grandson Aryeh Leib/Luis — Ashkenazi don't name for the living) `[L]` Narrative, p. 5; Tania never mentions meeting him `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`.
- Surname legend: house near a bridge → "Mostkov" `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`.
- Possibly an elder sister **Lyubka** (B09) `[?]` Narrative, p. 5 — placement disputed (CF-07).

### B02 · Chaya Sapotnitsky (Khaya; Sapotnisky/Saptonitsky)
- Surname is a **guesstimate** from Israel's Mexican death record "Israel Mostkoff Saptonitsky" (Mexican convention: mother's surname); no other confirmation `[?]` Narrative, pp. 4–5.
- b. 1837–62 (bounds from son Morduckh's 1877 birth); Wendy proposes ~1855–60 `[L]` Narrative, p. 4.
- "Lived all her life in a distant village — a place somewhere on the border with Poland, near **Neswalok**" (unidentified; Wendy's candidates: Sapotskin, Nesvizh/Nyasvizh, Nevolozh) `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`; see also Narrative, pp. 6–7.
- In Tania's childhood she lived in the Sadovaya St. household (corner of the bedroom; knitting; "small, quiet, very old") `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`.
- "Many children" — five named by Tania (Taiba, Motle, Hinde, Eizer + Israel) "and more"; "older brothers" and "uncles" in America imply further sons `TM (quoted Narrative, pp. 6, 11)`.
- d. Slutsk ~1917–18 of kidney disease (Tania 6–7 yrs old) `[L]` Narrative, p. 5; **not** found in the Slutsk Chevra Kadisha pinkas `[?]` Narrative, p. 9.
- Boonin cousins: TM says the Bunins were "cousins of Chaya" (see X01–X05; CF-10 on the Tsiptsin/Sapotnitsky spelling) Narrative, p. 27.

### B03 · Morduckh "Motl/Max" Mostkov (Mordukh; Mordoch)

### B04 · Israel Mostkoff (Isrol (Leibovich) Mostkov; "Israel Mostkoff Sapotnisky"; Israel Moskov)
- b. ~1878–80 Ostrov nr. Slutsk `[?]` — Narrative, p. 5 ("1878"); TM: born 1880 `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 6)`; **CF-01**.
- To Mississippi ~1895, age 15, with "older brothers" — peddlers, haberdashery, "their uncles were doing something for the owner" `TM (quoted Narrative, pp. 10–11)`; identity of brothers/uncles open `[?]` Narrative, p. 11.
- Returned to Slutsk 1905–08 `TM (quoted Narrative, p. 11)`; m. **Shifra Boruchovich** (C06) ~1907 (he ~25, she 27) `[L]` Narrative, p. 17; TM (quoted p. 17).
- Family lived on **Sadovaya Street**, Slutsk, house "owned by Wolfson", near the synagogue, across from the Slutsk river `[L]` Narrative, p. 12 (`TM`).
- Left Slutsk for Mexico 1925 (cart down Proletarskaya St.; "several other men also went"; unemployment/NEP-era taxes the driver — Slutsk Yizkor excerpt, Narrative, pp. 13–16). Mexico entry **1924** per immigration registration card & ship record (left Moscow 10 Dec 1924, arrived Vera Cruz on the *Edam*) `[C]` EV-02/03 — **CF-03** (1924 record vs. 1925 memoir).
- Headstone "Israel son of Aryeh Leib"; death record "Israel Mostkoff Sapotnisky" `[C]` Narrative, pp. 6, 23.
- d. 1 Oct 1957, Mexico City, "traumatic bronchopneumonia"; buried Panteon Israelita `[C]` Narrative, p. 20 — **CF-02** (memoir excerpt elsewhere says 1956).
