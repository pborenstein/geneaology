## The Tania Project — full text of Tania Mostkoff's memoir

- **Site**: https://www.tania-project.com/
- **Repo**: https://github.com/pborenstein/tania-project
- **What**: A digital memoir of **Tania (Tatyana / Татьяна Мосткова) Mostkoff**, daughter of Israel and Shifra Mostkoff — the sister who remained in the USSR when the family emigrated to Mexico (1928/29). Contains:
  - Russian transcription, OCR'd from photographs of the original book (`src/posts/russian.md`)
  - Full English translation (`src/posts/provisional-translation-en.md`)
  - Full Spanish translation (`src/posts/traduccion-provisional-es.md`)
  - Gazetteer mapping every place named in the memoir to modern names/countries/coordinates (`src/posts/locations.md`)
  - Scans of the original document, plus the original 2020 worklog
- **Why it matters**: This is a **primary source** for the Mostkoff line (Line B) and for Slutsk life 1911–1926. The genealogy corpus previously had only Wendy's annotated summary and excerpts ("Summarized Belarus memories of Tanya Mostkoff.pdf", "MOSTKOFF.pdf") — the full memoir is here.
- **Use in Phase 2**: Extraction pass 3 (Tanya material) should work from the site's English translation in addition to Wendy's summary, citing as `tania-project (section)`. The gazetteer is a ready-made starting point for the "world they lived in" section of the eventual publication.
